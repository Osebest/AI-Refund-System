# Refund Policy

1. Final-sale items are not eligible for refunds.
2. Orders older than 30 days from purchase date cannot be refunded.
3. Refunds above $500 require human review and receive status `escalated`.
4. Damaged or incorrect items may qualify for approval.
5. Suspicious or conflicting requests, such as a wrong-item claim when order notes say the item was delivered correctly, are escalated.

The deterministic policy engine is the source of truth. AI may classify language and identify suspicion, but it cannot override a denial or escalation produced by these rules.
